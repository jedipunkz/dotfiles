### Opening a PR

Invoked at the end of every other playbook.

Keep unrelated work out of the branch. Rebase into small, landable commits. Run the repository's formatter and linters over the diff before commit.

Follow the user's commit, branch, and PR rules and the **github-publish** skill for the title, body, and language. Name a real symbol in the title when one carries the change. Keep the body a one-minute briefing.

Stack follow-ups by base branch. Open PRs ready with `gh pr create` without `--draft`.
